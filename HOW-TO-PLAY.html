How to play Leap Frog

Same URL on phone, tablet, classroom PC, and laptop.

Goal

Hop the frog to the correct lily pad. Get 5 right answers to reach land.

Controls

DeviceLeftCenterRight
Phone / tabletTap left padTap center padTap right pad
Computer1 or ←2 or ↑3 or →

You can also tap the word on a pad.

Rules

  • Read the question above the three pads (left / center / right).
  • You have 15 seconds. Waiting too long sinks the home pad.
  • Right hop: jump + croak. Wrong hop or timeout: splash and lose a life.
  • Three lives. Five correct hops reach land.

Word packs

Words load from JSON (packs/starter-en.json), not a hardcoded list. Drop Excel / CSV / JSON on the start screen, or use ?pack=packs/starter.csv.

Audio

Baked local MP3/WAV files only. No server TTS and no browser speech. Sound starts when you tap Start jumping.
